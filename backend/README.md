Problem Statement

How might we design an AI-powered agent for the paints and coatings industry that automatically curates and delivers timely and project-driven company insights - sourced from financial data, construction activity, tenders, news, and social signals to sales representatives before client meetings, so they can identify paint demand opportunities, tailor product recommendations (e.g., decorative, waterproofing, industrial coatings), and significantly reduce manual research time while increasing sales rates?

Who are your customers?
The primary user of MASCoT is the sales representative employed by a paint manufacturer or distributor.

Key Differentiator vs. All Competitors
Every existing tool is industry-agnostic. MASCoT is the only solution purpose-built for the paint industry, delivering paint-specific signals (construction permits, OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) schedules, anti-corrosion regulation updates) with an authentic, verified paint sales lifecycle fed directly to the agent, giving minimum hallucination and maximum relevance.

Customer Value Proposition (CVP)

"For sales representatives of paint companies who want to identify and qualify the right clients before every meeting, MASCoT is a paint-industry-specific market scanning and lead discovery agent that helps them walk in confident and close deals faster by automating prospect research, financial analysis, and personalized outreach, we are exclusively focused on the paint industry, delivering pre-filtered, contextualized, and actionable insights."

Jobs-to-Be-Done (JTBD) Framework
Applying Clayton Christensen's JTBD model: "I hire MASCoT to...", the three job dimensions a paint sales rep is fulfilling:

Functional Jobs
Identify suitable market for product reach out
Detect early project signals
Identify companies likely to need paints
Track client financial & order history
Prioritize and score leads
Generate prospect insight reports
Automate cold outreach to leads
Map clients need to right product
Emotional Jobs
Feel confident before meetings
Avoid embarrassment from poor targeting
Reduce anxiety of manual research
Feel in control of their pipeline
Sense of professionalism vs peers
Social Jobs
Appear prepared and credible to clients
Be seen as top performer by management
Build reputation with large accounts
Demonstrate data-driven selling approach

Features:
Industry-level market scanning for paint leads
Automated company insights report per prospect
No manual research across multiple sources
Aggregates financial, news, and social media signals
Single summarized report per prospect
